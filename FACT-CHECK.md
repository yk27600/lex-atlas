# Lex Atlas fact-check log

The lessons for 156 countries were written by AI agents, then every claim was checked a second time against web sources (Wikipedia, constituteproject.org, official parliament and government sites, news agencies) by a separate set of AI agents. They corrected or removed anything wrong or unverifiable. The files below list what was changed in each batch of about 13 countries (alphabetical order), and anything that was kept without a firm source.

The 39 countries added earlier (Singapore to Ireland) were written from memory without this web check. Please have them reviewed by a person.

**Laws and governments change.** Several countries had coups, new constitutions or elections in 2025 and 2026 (for example Madagascar, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Venezuela, Zimbabwe, Syria, Myanmar and Nicaragua). Those lessons use "as of" wording. This log reflects what could be found in October 2026. A teacher or law student should still review the lessons before real students use them.

- Confirmed and kept: AF, AL, DZ, AD, AT, BS, BH, BB (Parliament 1639, buildings 1870-74, President 4-year term, Senate 21), AZ referendum 2016, BS 2016 referendum and 1729.
